## Summary
- 41 model/setting rows. A÷B median 1.02, min 0.92 (vith16plus 512px b1), max 1.81 (bert-base S128 b1).
- A broken: none. A needed autocast fp16 instead of hard half in 2 rows (see A precision). B missing: none (all families have a friendly variant here). SAM 3 / SAM 3.1 are in 007-meta-vision/results/2026-10-12_algo_only.md (sam3-x image row); SAM 3 video / 3.1 not run.
- Almost all of B's gain over a properly switched A is cheap or absent (ratio about 1.0 for DINOv2 / DINOv3 / ConvNeXt / large CNNs, i.e. the 2026-10-10 "friendly" speed-ups came from fp16 + fp16-accumulate + TF32, which A now gets too). The exceptions are small-batch BERT / CLIP-text (about 1.7-1.9x at S128 b1, [cheap] overhead removal) and small-M CNNs (efficientnet_b0 / mobilenetv2, about 1.15-1.2x, BN folding).
